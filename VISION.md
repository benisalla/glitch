# Glitch: vision

**Glitch the autopilot. Look, then tap.**

Your phone is designed to be tapped without thinking. The thumb knows where every icon lives, and one unlock to check the time turns into forty minutes of scrolling. Glitch is a small, kind interruption in that loop. Today it shows your apps in a new order every time you open it, so you look before you choose. Next, it grows into a set of gentle cues you set up yourself.

We are building Glitch with ADHD and fast-wandering attention in mind. That shapes every decision:

- **Your words, not ours.** Reminders are sentences you write, shown back to you at the moment they matter.
- **Always an exit.** No lockouts, no streaks, no guilt. "Open anyway" and "pause for today" are always one tap away.
- **Kind and rare.** Glitch must never become another thing that interrupts you.
- **Private by design.** No internet, no accounts, no analytics. What you do stays on your phone.
- **Honest.** Glitch is not a medical device and does not treat ADHD or any condition. It helps you notice the moment; what you do with it is your call.

## Where it is going

The long-term goal is the **Reels Glitch**. When you've slipped into endless short videos (Reels, Shorts, TikTok), Glitch steps in with a reminder you wrote for yourself, and gives you an easy way out.

We are getting there in steps, so trust comes before permissions:

```
shuffle  →  intention moment  →  gentle time cues  →  Reels Glitch
 (today)       (v1.1)               (v1.2–v2.0)        (future add-on)
```
