# Honest lines for store copy

Ready-to-use lines. Source numbers refer to the table in `research.md`. Rules: no claims about focus, memory, intelligence, health or screen time. Never put a study's numbers next to the app name.

| # | Line | Relies on |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Your apps, in a new order every time you open the menu." | Describes the app; no source needed. |
| 2 | "No more tapping by position: read the list to find the app you want." | Describes the app. A changing order removes position-based speed (#4, #5, #6). |
| 3 | "Breaks the habit of tapping apps from muscle memory." | #4 (disrupting an interface habit removes its automatic speed advantage), #5, #6. Keep "tapping apps"; don't widen it to "phone habits". |
| 4 | "A small moment of friction before you open an app." | #5, #6 (a changing order adds a search step); #9 (design friction / microboundaries). Don't say it reduces use. |
| 5 | "Inspired by research on habits and design friction." | #2, #3 (habits depend on stable cues), #9 (design friction). Background only; never "proven" or "science-backed". |
| 6 | "Tap an app and it opens normally. No timers, no blockers, no nagging." | Describes the app; no source needed. |
| 7 | "Back to the menu? Fresh order." | Describes the app (a new session reshuffles); no source needed. |
| 8 | "No permissions. No internet. No tracking. Your app list never leaves your phone." | Describes the app (manifest and AGENTS.md privacy rules); no source needed. |
| 9 | "Keeps your home screen and app drawer exactly as they are. Shuffle Menu is a separate menu you open when you want it." | Describes the app (AGENTS.md platform boundary); no source needed. |
| 10 | "Takes a few seconds longer to find an app. That's the point." | #5, #6 (a changing order is slower). Honest about the trade-off. |

## Do not use (see `research.md` for the reasons)
"improves focus", "boosts memory", "brain training", "reduces screen time", "use your phone X% less", "science-backed / clinically proven", "beat phone addiction", "digital detox", "more mindful", "better sleep", "less anxiety".
