Welcome to Glitch 1.0.0, the first release.
• Your apps appear in a new random order every time you open the menu.
• Tap any app to open it as usual. Come back for a fresh order.
• The order holds still while you look, even when you rotate the phone.
• New or removed apps update the list without reshuffling the rest.
• Light and dark themes that follow your system setting.
• No permissions, no internet, no ads, no tracking.
