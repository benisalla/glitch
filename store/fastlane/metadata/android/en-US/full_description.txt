Your apps, in a new order every time you open the menu.

Shuffle Menu shows the apps on your phone with their usual icons and names, in a random order. Each time you open it, the order is new. So instead of tapping a spot from memory, you look at the list and choose. A small change to a moment you repeat many times a day.

How it works
1. Open Shuffle Menu from your home screen.
2. Your apps appear in a fresh, shuffled order.
3. Look, then tap. The app opens just like it always does.

Come back to the menu and the order changes again.

Why shuffle?
Most of us open apps by position. The thumb knows where an icon lives and taps before we have really decided. That is handy, and it is also a habit that runs on its own.

Shuffle Menu gently breaks that pattern. When the icons are never in the same place twice, you read the names and pick the app you actually came for. It takes a second, and that second is yours.

Steady while you look
• The order stays put while the menu is on screen. It does not jump around, even when you rotate the phone.
• Install or remove an app while the menu is open, and only that app is added or removed. Everything else stays where it was.
• Light and dark themes follow your system setting.

Private by design
• No permissions requested.
• No internet access, so nothing can leave your phone.
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts.
• Your app list is read on your phone to draw the menu, and stays there.
• It only sees apps that show up in a launcher, not every package on your device.

What it doesn't do
• It does not replace your home screen or your app drawer. Both keep working exactly as before.
• No reminders, blockers, timers or usage statistics.
• It does not lock, hide or limit any app. It only changes the order of the list.

FAQ
Does it replace my home screen?
No. Shuffle Menu is a normal app you open when you want it. Your home screen and app drawer stay the same.

How do I keep it handy?
Long-press Shuffle Menu in your app drawer and add it to your home screen.

Does the order change while I'm looking at it?
No. You get one new order each time you open the menu. It stays the same until you leave and come back.

Why does it need no permissions?
Android lets an app see the apps that appear in a launcher without any special permission. That is all Shuffle Menu needs.

Are all my apps listed?
Every app you can open from your app drawer is listed. Apps inside a work profile or a private or secure folder are not shown.

Does it cost anything?
No. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.
