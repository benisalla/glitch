Glitch the autopilot. Look, then tap.

Glitch shows the apps on your phone, with their usual icons and names, in a random order. Every time you open it, the order is reshuffled. Your thumb can't tap a spot from memory, so you look at the list and choose. One small glitch in a moment you repeat many times a day.

How it works
1. Open Glitch from your home screen.
2. Your apps appear in a fresh, shuffled order.
3. Look, then tap. The app opens just like it always does.

Come back to Glitch and the order changes again.

A widget for your home screen
• Add the Shuffled apps widget to your usual home screen: long-press the home screen, tap Widgets, then Glitch.
• It shows your apps in a shuffled order. Tap one and it opens normally.
• The order changes after each tap from the widget, each time you open Glitch, and from time to time on its own.
• Resize it to show more apps, or scroll inside it.

Why glitch?
Most of us open apps by position. The thumb knows where an icon lives and taps before we have really decided. That is handy, and it is also a habit that runs on autopilot.

Glitch breaks tapping by position. When the icons are rarely in the same place twice, you read the names and pick the app you actually came for. It takes a second longer to find an app. That's the point: you get a second to choose.

A dock for everyday apps
• Up to 4 apps stay in a fixed dock and are left out of the shuffle. The defaults are phone, messages, camera and browser.
• Long-press any app to add it to the dock or remove it. Long-press also opens App info.

Steady while you look
• The order stays put while Glitch is on screen. It does not jump around, even when you rotate the phone.
• Install or remove an app, and only that app is added or removed. Everything else stays where it was.
• Light and dark themes follow your system setting.

Private by design
• No permissions requested.
• No internet access, so nothing can leave your phone.
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts.
• Your app list is read on your phone to draw the menu, and stays there.
• It only sees apps that show up in a launcher, not every package on your device.

What it doesn't do
• It never replaces your home screen or touches your phone's launcher or app drawer. Both keep working exactly as before. The widget sits on your home screen like any other widget.
• No reminders, blockers, timers or usage statistics.
• It does not lock, hide or limit any app. It only changes the order of the list.

FAQ
Does it replace my home screen?
No. Glitch is a normal app you open when you want it. Your home screen and app drawer stay the same.

How do I keep it handy?
Long-press Glitch in your app drawer and add it to your home screen. Or add the Shuffled apps widget.

Does the order change while I'm looking at it?
No. You get one new order each time you open Glitch. It stays the same until you leave and come back.

Why does it need no permissions?
Android lets an app see the apps that appear in a launcher without any special permission. That is all Glitch needs.

Are all my apps listed?
Every app you can open from your app drawer is listed. Apps inside a work profile or a private or secure folder are not shown.

Does it cost anything?
No. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.
