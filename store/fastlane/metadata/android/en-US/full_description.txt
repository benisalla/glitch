Shuffle App Menu shows the apps installed on your phone in a random order. Every time you open it, the order is new, so you read the list to find what you want instead of tapping a spot from habit.

HOW IT WORKS
• Open Shuffle Menu from your home screen or app drawer.
• Your apps appear with their usual icon and name, in a shuffled order.
• Tap an app to open it, exactly as you normally would.
• Come back to the menu and you get a fresh order.

WHAT STAYS THE SAME
• The order does not jump around while you are looking at it. Rotating the phone keeps the same order.
• If you install or remove an app while the menu is open, that app is added or removed and everything else stays where it was.

WHAT IT IS NOT
• It is a separate app, not a home-screen (launcher) replacement. Your phone's home screen and app drawer keep working as before.
• It has no reminders, blockers, timers, usage tracking or accounts.
• It makes no claims about focus, memory or productivity. It only changes the order of the list.

PRIVACY
• No permissions requested. No internet access.
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts.
• The list of your apps is read on your phone and never leaves it.
• To find apps it can open, it uses Android's narrow "launcher apps" visibility, not access to every package on the device.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Apps in a work profile or in Samsung Secure Folder are not listed.
• Tip: long-press Shuffle Menu in your app drawer and add it to your home screen for quick access.
