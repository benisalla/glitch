# Privacy Policy for Glitch

**Effective date:** 2026-10-13

Glitch ("the app", package `com.benisalla.glitch`) is an Android app that shows the apps installed on your phone in a random order. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: **it collects nothing, and nothing leaves your phone.**

## What the app uses
To build its menu, the app asks Android for the list of apps that can be opened from a launcher (their name and icon). It uses Android's narrow "launcher apps" visibility for this, not access to every package on the device.

This list is used only on your phone, only while the app is running, to show the menu and open the app you tap. It is not saved, not sent anywhere and not shared with anyone.

## What the app does not do
- It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal or device data.
- It has no internet access, so it cannot send data off your phone.
- It requests no Android permissions.
- It has no ads, analytics, crash reporting, tracking or third-party SDKs that collect data.
- It has no accounts or sign-in.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
